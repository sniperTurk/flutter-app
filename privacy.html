Konum (yalnızca "Hava & Rüzgâr"). Konumunuz yalnızca siz hava verisini getirdiğinizde ve izin verdiğinizde, bulunduğunuz yerin hava tahminini almak için kullanılır. Hareket sensörü ve pusula. Su Terazisi ve Pusula araçları cihaz sensörlerini yalnızca ekranda göstermek için okur; veri saklanmaz veya gönderilmez.

Balistik uyarısı. Uygulama bir hesap yardımcısıdır; sonuçlar gerçek atış doğrulamasının yerini tutmaz.

Haklarınız ve iletişim. Bizde sizinle ilişkilendirilmiş kişisel veri tutulmadığından silinecek bir hesap verisi yoktur; cihazdaki verileri uygulamayı silerek kaldırabilirsiniz. Sorularınız için: Sekerbirol76@gmail.com

English

In short: SNIPER TÜRK has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and does not send data that identifies you to any server of ours.

Stored on your device. Your rifle, ammunition and scope profiles, settings and manual catalog entries stay on your device only and are never sent to us or to third parties. Deleting the app deletes them.

Location (Weather & Wind only). When you ask for weather data and allow location access, your location is used to fetch the forecast. Coordinates rounded to 4 decimal places (about 10 metres) are sent to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway, api.met.no). The app does not store your location. As with any internet request, MET Norway's servers can see your IP address, and MET Norway's own privacy terms apply. Without location permission every other tool still works.

Camera and photo library (Sight Height only). The single photo you take or pick is processed in device memory; it is not saved, not written to your photo library and does not leave your device. The app does not record audio.

Motion sensors and compass. The Level and Compass tools read device sensors only to display values on screen; nothing is stored or sent.

What we do not do. No advertising ID, tracking, profiling, selling of data or sharing of data with third parties. The app is not directed at children.

Ballistics notice. The app is a calculation aid and does not replace real-world verification.

Your rights and contact. We hold no personal data linked to you, so there is no account data to delete; remove on-device data by deleting the app. Questions: Sekerbirol76@gmail.com
